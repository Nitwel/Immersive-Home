## Interaction

Immersive Home can be used with controllers or with your hands. As soon as you put the controllers down, the app switches to hand tracking. Your virtual hands are only shown while hand tracking is active, so they are hidden while you hold the controllers.

### Clicking

Clicking can be done in two ways:

- **From a distance:** point at a button and pinch your index finger and thumb together.
- **By touch:** press a button with your index finger, like you would in the real world. Buttons only react when your finger comes from the front, so reaching through a panel doesn't press the buttons behind it.

You can also touch a light bulb with your finger to turn the light on or off.

### Moving

You can move objects by pinching your middle finger and thumb together, or, when you're close enough, also by pinching your index finger and thumb together. Let go to drop the object.

### Zooming the Map View

Pinch the [Map View](/getting-started/map-view) with both hands and pull them apart or push them together to zoom in or out.

### Opening the menu

The menu can be opened and closed by pressing index and thumb finger together while pointing both fingers in the direction of your headset.

### Palm quick actions

Turn your left palm towards your face to show the quick action buttons above it:

- **Map:** opens or closes the [Map View](/getting-started/map-view).
- **Temperature:** turns the temperature map on or off.
- **Humidity:** turns the humidity map on or off.

### Widget settings

Point at a widget with your left hand. A settings button appears on your hand; press it to open the widget's settings.

### Typing

The virtual keyboard has a standard QWERTY layout. **Shift** applies to the next character, **Caps** keeps capital letters on, and the arrow keys move the cursor. **Enter** confirms the input, and the QR code button scans text from a QR code. In text fields you can double-click to select a word and triple-click to select everything.
